# Kenchain Groups website media sources

The photos and video added to the website load from their source CDN URLs at runtime; media files are not bundled locally in this archive. Visitors need an internet connection to load these assets.

## Video
- Resort drone footage by Grisha Grishkoff on Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/video/drone-shot-of-hotel-and-resort-with-a-mountain-view-4185232/
- Pexels content license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

## Photos
- Hospitality, hotel and resort images are served from Unsplash image CDN (`images.unsplash.com`) by photo ID in `frontend/index.html` and `frontend/js/main.js`.
- Travel, lake, beach and mountain images are served from Unsplash image CDN (`images.unsplash.com`) by photo ID in `frontend/js/main.js`.
- Courier parcel image: Pexels photo “Stacks of Boxes Ready for Delivery”: https://www.pexels.com/photo/stacks-of-boxes-ready-for-delivery-4440800/
- Unsplash license: https://unsplash.com/license

Pexels states that photos and videos can be used for free on websites and that attribution is not required. Unsplash's license similarly permits broad free usage for images, subject to its license restrictions. Review provider terms and any model/property rights where appropriate before commercial launch.
