05 / OUR CRAFT

From Material
to Masterpiece.

Explore the processes, materials and details that contribute to the creation of furniture and cabinetry.

01
STAGE 01

Material Selection

Careful evaluation of solid timber species, moisture content, high-density core panels, and architectural veneers chosen for longevity and stability in Indian climate conditions.

02
STAGE 02

Precision Processing

Accurate panel sizing, calibrated routing, and clean edge-banding utilizing specialized woodworking machinery to ensure millimeter-accurate cabinet components.

03
STAGE 03

Cabinet Fabrication

Structural carcass assembly, dowelled and reinforced joinery, internal partition alignment, and rigorous dry-fitting of hardware, hinges, and drawer runners.

04
STAGE 04

Finishing & Detailing

Multi-stage progressive surface sanding, moisture-resistant sealing, hand-applied stains, protective polyurethane coats, and meticulous hardware inspection.

05
STAGE 05

Finished Spaces

Carefully managed site delivery, leveled installation, architectural panel alignment, and final handover of living spaces ready for generations of use.
