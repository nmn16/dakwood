A / THE MANUFACTURING BASE

Dakwood Furniture & Interiors

Dakwood Furniture and Interiors Private Limited is based in Udupi, Karnataka. Its reported product focus includes wooden furniture, modular kitchens, wardrobes and interior solutions, supported by an operating manufacturing unit and woodworking machinery.
