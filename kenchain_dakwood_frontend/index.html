B / THE MANUFACTURING BASE

About Dakwood Furniture & Interiors

Dakwood Furniture and Interiors Private Limited is based in Udupi, Karnataka. Its specialized product focus includes solid-wood and engineered-wood furniture, modular kitchens, wardrobes and built-in storage, decorative wall panels, ceiling finishes, TV stands, and display showcases. Supported by an operating manufacturing facility and modern woodworking machinery, Dakwood connects raw materials, precision machining, and custom finishes into cohesive interior solutions.
